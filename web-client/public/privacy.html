Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 2024

SudoDuel ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information:

  • Email address (if using email login)
  • Display name you choose
  • Authentication data from Apple Sign-In or Google Sign-In (if used)

Game Data:

  • Match history and results
  • Rating and ranking
  • Game statistics (wins, losses, completion times)

Device Information:

  • Device type and operating system
  • App version

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the game service
  • To match you with other players
  • To track your progress, rating, and statistics
  • To display leaderboards
  • To enforce our Terms of Service
  • To respond to support requests

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Your display name and rating are visible to other players. We may share information with:

  • Service providers who help operate our service (hosting, analytics)
  • Law enforcement when required by law

4. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from the app settings, which will permanently remove all your data within 30 days.

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit and at rest. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Update your display name

7. Children's Privacy

SudoDuel is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
